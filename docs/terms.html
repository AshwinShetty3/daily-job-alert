Purpose and operator

Daily Job Alert is a personal job-search automation operated by Ashwin Shetty. This website documents the application; it does not provide a public hosted subscription service.

Authorized use

Use the application only with accounts and infrastructure you own or are authorized to manage. Follow each data provider's terms and quotas. Keep credentials private and configure the intended digest recipient carefully. Gmail data handling is described in the privacy policy.

Listings and availability

Listings belong to their respective providers and employers. Dates, experience requirements and locations may be incomplete or inaccurate, and automated parsing can miss details. Verify requirements, eligibility and application links with the employer. The tool does not apply for jobs or guarantee employment, complete coverage, duplicate-free delivery or exact delivery times.

Costs and stopping use

The project is designed to use free services and quotas. Providers control their pricing and availability; operators are responsible for any services they choose to enable. Stop use by disabling the workflow and revoking account access. There is no paid subscription offered by this website.
